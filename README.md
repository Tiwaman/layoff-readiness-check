# Layoff Readiness Check

A free, 4-minute self-assessment that shows how exposed your job is to layoffs, how strong your safety net is, and what to do next.

- 23 questions across your company, your role, you at work, and your finances
- Two scores: job exposure and safety net, plotted on a simple four-zone chart
- A personal action plan for this week, this month, and the next 90 days
- No sign-up, no tracking: answers never leave the browser

This is a self-assessment based on common layoff patterns, not a prediction.

## Run it
It's a single `index.html` file. Open it in any browser, or deploy it as a static site (for example on Vercel).
